# DeepSeek-R1: Incentivizing Reasoning Capability in LLMs via Reinforcement Learning

**arXiv:** [2501.12948](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12948)
**Date submitted:** January 22, 2025 (v1); January 4, 2026 (v2)
**Venue:** **Nature** 645:633-638 (2025) — full peer-reviewed publication
**Authors:** DeepSeek-AI team (200+ contributors); lead authors include Daya Guo, Dejian Yang

## Summary

Demonstrates that frontier reasoning capability — competitive with OpenAI's o1 — can be elicited from base LLMs by **pure reinforcement learning on verifiable rewards (RLVR)**, without any human-labeled chain-of-thought traces. The paper introduces two models (DeepSeek-R1-Zero, trained from base via RL only; DeepSeek-R1, with a four-stage refinement pipeline) and distills their reasoning into six smaller dense models. This is the first peer-reviewed demonstration that emergent reasoning patterns — self-reflection, verification, dynamic strategy adaptation — can arise without supervised reasoning data.

## Key Takeaways

### Two complementary models

- **DeepSeek-R1-Zero**: Trained directly from DeepSeek-V3-Base via large-scale RL with **no SFT cold-start**. Demonstrates pure-RL reasoning is possible. Suffers readability and language-mixing issues.
- **DeepSeek-R1**: Adds a four-stage pipeline (cold-start data → reasoning RL → rejection sampling for SFT → generic RL) on top of R1-Zero's insights. Matches OpenAI-o1-1217 on reasoning benchmarks while being readable.

### GRPO — Group Relative Policy Optimization

Replaces PPO's per-token value model with **group-relative advantages**: sample G outputs per prompt, normalize their rewards within the group, use the normalized advantage as the policy gradient signal. Eliminates the value-function critic, halving memory cost.

For a group of G sampled outputs $\{o_1, ..., o_G\}$ with rewards $\{r_1, ..., r_G\}$:

$$A_i = \frac{r_i - \text{mean}(\{r_1, ..., r_G\})}{\text{std}(\{r_1, ..., r_G\})}$$

The GRPO objective:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{GRPO}} = \mathbb{E}\left[\frac{1}{G}\sum_{i=1}^{G} \min\left(\rho_i A_i, \text{clip}(\rho_i, 1-\epsilon, 1+\epsilon) A_i\right) - \beta \text{KL}(\pi_\theta || \pi_{\text{ref}})\right]$$

### Reward design — the RLVR insight

Rewards are **rule-based and verifiable**, not learned from human preference:
- **Accuracy reward**: did the final answer match ground truth (math) or pass tests (code)?
- **Format reward**: does the output enclose reasoning in `<think>...</think>` tags before the answer?

This sidesteps the **reward-hacking** failure mode of PPO+RM pipelines and avoids the cost of preference annotation.

### Emergent reasoning behaviors

R1-Zero exhibits **"aha moments"** during training — autonomously discovering self-verification, backtracking, and exploration of alternative approaches. The paper shows response length grows monotonically during training as the model learns to think longer on harder problems.

### Benchmark results (DeepSeek-R1)

- **AIME 2024**: 79.8% (vs o1-1217's 79.2%)
- **MATH-500**: 97.3% (vs o1-1217's 96.4%)
- **Codeforces**: 96.3 percentile (Elo 2029)
- **MMLU**: 90.8%

### Distillation matters

The team distills R1 into **six dense models** (Qwen-1.5B/7B/14B/32B, Llama-8B/70B). Distilled-Qwen-32B outperforms o1-mini on multiple reasoning benchmarks — challenging the assumption that small models can't reason.
