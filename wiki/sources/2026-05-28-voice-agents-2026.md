## Summary

A consolidated 2026 snapshot of the [[Voice Agents]] field: the two dominant architectures (cascaded STT→LLM→TTS pipelines vs native end-to-end speech-to-speech), latency benchmarks, the TTS/STT leaderboard, and the engineering concerns that determine production quality (barge-in, endpointing, transport).

## Key Claims

- **Two architectures** dominate: **cascaded** (STT → LLM → TTS, model-agnostic, cheaper, telephony-friendly) and **native S2S** (single audio-in/audio-out model, preserves prosody, lower latency in best case).
- **Sub-1s end-to-end TTFT is table stakes** as of April 2026 — xAI Grok Voice (0.78s), OpenAI gpt-realtime-1.5 (0.82s), Amazon Nova 2 Sonic (1.14s), Gemini 3.1 Flash Live (2.98s).
- **Inworld Realtime TTS 1.5 Max** leads the TTS leaderboard (~1208 ELO, P90 < 250ms TTFA).
- **Cartesia Sonic 3.5** has the lowest TTFA (40ms) via its State Space Model architecture.
- **Hume EVI** specializes in **emotional prosody** — adjusts response delivery based on detected user emotion.
- Critical engineering challenges: **barge-in** (~100ms cancellation), **endpointing** (VAD vs prosody-based vs model-based), **transport** (WebSocket vs WebRTC vs SIP).
- **Function calling in realtime** is less mature than in text LLMs; patterns include speculative speech, parallel tool calls, cached responses.
