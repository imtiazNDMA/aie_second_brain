# Instagram Publishing Log

Use one row per post. Record raw metrics and the lesson they imply; do not optimize from one post alone.

|  ID | Published | Topic | Format | Reach | Saves | Shares | Comments | Profile visits | Follows | Main lesson |
| --: | --------- | ----- | ------ | ----: | ----: | -----: | -------: | -------------: | ------: | ----------- |

## Review formulas

```text
Save rate = saves / reach
Share rate = shares / reach
Conversation rate = qualified comments and DMs / reach
Follow conversion = follows / profile visits
```

## 48-hour review

- Did the hook attract the intended audience?
- Where did readers misunderstand the lesson?
- Which slide was quoted, saved, or discussed?
- Did the comments reveal a stronger follow-up topic?

## 14-day review

- Did the post continue to earn saves and shares?
- Did it bring qualified followers or only broad reach?
- Which hook, structure, and visual pattern should be reused?
- What should change in [[Carousel Writing Standard]] or [[AI Engineering Carousel Backlog]]?

## Monthly review

Review a minimum of 12 posts before drawing broad conclusions. Compare performance by content pillar, level, hook type, slide count, and visual direction.
