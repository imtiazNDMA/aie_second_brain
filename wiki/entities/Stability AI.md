# Stability AI

## Identity

Founded in 2020, headquartered in London. The org that funded and released **Stable Diffusion** in August 2022 — the watershed moment that made high-quality open-weight text-to-image generation broadly available. The original Stable Diffusion research came from the **CompVis** group at LMU Munich (Robin Rombach, Andreas Blattmann, et al.) and was sponsored / released by Stability AI.

After significant turmoil in 2024 (founder departure, financial restructuring), many of the original SD authors left to form [[Black Forest Labs]]. Stability AI continued the SD lineage with [[Stable Diffusion 3]] and SD 3.5 in 2024.

## Significance

- **Made open-weight text-to-image happen** — Stable Diffusion's release was the inflection point.
- **Latent diffusion paradigm** — the SD architecture is now the default recipe for image/video generation.
- **Ecosystem effects** — ControlNet, LoRA, IP-Adapter, AnimateDiff all built on SD weights.
- Continued the lineage with SD3 / SD3.5 after founders departed.
