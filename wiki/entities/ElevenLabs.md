ElevenLabs is an AI voice synthesis platform specializing in high-quality text-to-speech (TTS) and voice cloning technology. It enables developers to create natural-sounding, expressive voices for conversational agents and content generation.

## Key Features
- Ultra-realistic text-to-speech with natural prosody and emotional range
- Voice cloning from short audio samples for custom voice creation
- Low-latency streaming TTS for real-time voice agent applications
- Multi-language support with consistent voice quality across languages
- Voice design API for fine-tuning tone, pace, and style
