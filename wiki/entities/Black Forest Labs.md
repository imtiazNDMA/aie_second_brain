# Black Forest Labs

## Identity

German AI lab founded in 2024 by **Robin Rombach** (first author of the original Stable Diffusion paper) and other lead authors of the latent-diffusion research line. Headquartered in the Black Forest region (Freiburg / southwest Germany). Releases the **[[Flux.1]]** family of text-to-image models — flagship open- and closed-weight diffusion models. Quickly became the open-weight T2I leader displacing Stable Diffusion XL.

## Models released

| Model | Year | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Flux.1 [pro] | 2024 | Closed API, flagship quality |
| Flux.1 [dev] | 2024 | Open-weight, non-commercial |
| Flux.1 [schnell] | 2024 | Open-weight Apache-2, 1–4 step distilled |
| Flux.1 [Fill] / [Depth] / [Canny] | 2024–2025 | ControlNet-style guidance models |

## Significance

- **Continued the Stable Diffusion lineage** outside Stability AI.
- **Validated MMDiT + Rectified Flow** at scale.
- **Best-in-class typography** in generated images at launch.
