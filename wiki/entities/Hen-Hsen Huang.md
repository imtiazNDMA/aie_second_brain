# Hen-Hsen Huang

## Identity

Researcher at the Institute of Information Science, Academia Sinica, Taiwan. Corresponding author of **"Don't Do RAG: When Cache-Augmented Generation is All You Need for Knowledge Tasks"** (WWW 2025), which introduced [[Cache-Augmented Generation]] as an alternative to RAG for bounded-corpus knowledge tasks.

## Research focus

- Long-context language models
- Retrieval and information access in LLMs
- Knowledge-grounded text generation
