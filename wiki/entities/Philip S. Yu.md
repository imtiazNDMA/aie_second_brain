# Philip S. Yu

## Identity

Distinguished Professor of Computer Science at the University of Illinois Chicago (UIC). One of the most cited researchers in computer science (h-index well above 200), with foundational contributions across data mining, database systems, knowledge discovery, and — most recently — large language models in IT operations.

ACM Fellow, IEEE Fellow, AAAS Fellow. Recipient of the IEEE Computer Society Technical Achievement Award and ACM SIGKDD Innovation Award.

## Research footprint

Yu's career spans four decades of foundational work:

- **Data mining and KDD** — early frequent-pattern mining, classification, clustering at IBM Research
- **Information retrieval and recommendation** — collaborative filtering, web mining
- **Graph mining and social networks** — community detection, anomaly detection in graphs
- **Heterogeneous information networks** — meta-path-based analysis
- **Recent LLM/AIOps work** — co-author on the 2025 ACM Computing Surveys synthesis of LLM-based AIOps
