# Niklas Muennighoff

## Identity

PhD researcher at Stanford University. Lead author of **s1: Simple test-time scaling** (EMNLP 2025), the most reproducible public recipe for reasoning-model training. Prior contributions span open-source LLM evaluation and multilingual NLP (lead author of MTEB benchmark; contributor to BLOOM and StarCoder lines).

## Significance

Muennighoff's s1 paper demonstrated that the o1-style reasoning paradigm can be replicated with **1,000 carefully curated training examples and a generation-time string controller** — challenging the assumption that frontier reasoning required massive RL infrastructure. The work made test-time compute scaling broadly accessible.

## Notable contributions

- **s1 / s1K** — 1,000-example dataset and budget-forcing technique for reasoning models
- **MTEB** — Massive Text Embedding Benchmark, the standard evaluation suite for sentence-embedding models
- Contributions to BLOOM, StarCoder, OctoPack lines of open models
