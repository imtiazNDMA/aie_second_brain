# Lingzhe Zhang

## Identity

Lead author of **"A Survey of AIOps in the Era of Large Language Models"** (ACM Computing Surveys, accepted; arXiv:2507.12472). The survey reviewed 183 peer-reviewed papers from 2020–2024 to consolidate the emerging field of [[LLM4AIOps]] and distinguish it from [[LLMOps]].

## Significance

Zhang's survey provides the field-defining taxonomy for LLM-based AIOps: how LLMs change available data sources, how operational tasks are reframed as conversation tasks, what techniques are deployed in practice, and where evaluation gaps remain. The work appears in **ACM Computing Surveys** — the canonical venue for surveys in computing — alongside foundational researcher [[Philip S. Yu]] as senior author.
