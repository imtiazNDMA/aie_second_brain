Deepgram is a cloud-based speech-to-text and voice AI platform that provides fast, accurate transcription and understanding of spoken language. It offers real-time streaming transcription, speaker diarization, and sentiment analysis, making it a core component in voice agent architectures.

## Key Features
- Real-time streaming transcription with low latency for live conversations
- Automatic speech recognition (ASR) with high accuracy across multiple languages
- Speaker diarization to identify and separate multiple speakers in audio
- Natural language understanding (NLU) integration for extracting meaning from speech
- Voice agent framework support for building conversational AI systems
