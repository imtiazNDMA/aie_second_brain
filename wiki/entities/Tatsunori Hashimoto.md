# Tatsunori Hashimoto

## Identity

Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Stanford University, focused on robust and trustworthy machine learning. Senior author of **s1: Simple test-time scaling** (EMNLP 2025) and a recurring contributor to research on language-model alignment, evaluation, and societal effects of large models.

## Research focus

- Reasoning models and test-time compute (s1 line)
- Robustness of language models to distribution shift
- Alignment and human-feedback techniques
- Evaluation methodologies for generative models
