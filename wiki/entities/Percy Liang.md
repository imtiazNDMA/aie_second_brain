# Percy Liang

## Identity

Associate Professor of Computer Science at Stanford University and Director of the Center for Research on Foundation Models (CRFM). One of the foundational figures in modern NLP and foundation-model research, with seminal contributions to evaluation, robustness, and societal impact of large models.

## Research focus

- Foundation models — capabilities, evaluation, governance (lead author of *On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models*, 2021)
- HELM — Holistic Evaluation of Language Models, the broadest standardized LLM benchmark
- Reasoning models and test-time compute (co-author on s1)
- Semantic parsing, question answering, robust ML

## Significance

Liang's CRFM at Stanford has shaped the academic conversation around foundation models — both technical (HELM benchmark, Alpaca line) and policy (foundation-model governance, NIST AISI involvement). Most major NLP/foundation-model research at Stanford traces a thread to CRFM.
