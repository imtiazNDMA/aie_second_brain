# Sebastian Raschka

Machine learning researcher and author known for practical deep learning education. Published "Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch)" — an implementation-focused guide to LLMs.

According to the author bio in *Build a Reasoning Model (From Scratch)* (Manning, 2026):
- **Roles.** He is an LLM Research Engineer. He was previously a senior engineer at Lightning AI and a statistics professor at the University of Wisconsin–Madison.
- **Other books.** *Machine Learning with PyTorch and Scikit-Learn* and *Machine Learning Q and AI*.
- **Writing.** He publishes at magazine.sebastianraschka.com.
- **Code.** The book's code is at github.com/rasbt/reasoning-from-scratch.

**What the 2026 book covers.** It is a sequel to the LLM book. It takes a pretrained [[Qwen3]] 0.6B base model and turns it into a reasoning model using three method families:
- inference-time scaling ([[Self-Consistency]], [[Self-Refinement]]);
- reinforcement learning ([[RLVR]], [[GRPO]]);
- [[Distillation]].

Each step is evaluated on [[MATH-500]].
