# Brian J Chan

## Identity

Lead author of **"Don't Do RAG: When Cache-Augmented Generation is All You Need for Knowledge Tasks"** (WWW 2025). Researcher at National Taiwan University working at the intersection of long-context language models and retrieval systems.

## Significance

First-author on the paper that introduced [[Cache-Augmented Generation]] as a paradigm-level alternative to [[Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] for bounded-corpus knowledge tasks. The work has been influential in shaping the post-2024 conversation about whether RAG is required when long-context windows are sufficient.
