# Mistral AI

## Identity

French AI lab founded in April 2023 by ex-DeepMind / ex-Meta researchers (Arthur Mensch, Guillaume Lample, Timothée Lacroix). Based in Paris. Released **Mistral 7B** (Sep 2023), **[[Mixtral]] 8x7B** (Dec 2023, first popular open MoE), **Mixtral 8x22B** (2024), **Pixtral 12B** (2024, VLM), Codestral, Ministral, and the closed flagship **Mistral Large 2**.

## Significance

- **Champion of European AI sovereignty** — major non-US, non-Chinese player.
- **Apache-2 licensing default** — most permissive among major labs.
- **Mixtral broke open MoE.** Before Mixtral, MoE was niche; after, it became mainstream.
- **Pixtral** brought open-weight VLMs into the Mistral ecosystem.
