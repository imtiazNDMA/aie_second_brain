# Khanh-Tung Tran

## Identity

Researcher at University College Cork (Ireland), affiliated with the Insight Centre for Data Analytics. Lead author of **"Multi-Agent Collaboration Mechanisms: A Survey of LLMs"** (arXiv:2501.06322, 2025), which established the canonical 2025 taxonomy for classifying LLM-based multi-agent systems.

## Significance

Tran's survey provides the five-axis classification (Actors × Types × Structures × Strategies × Coordination Protocols) that lets practitioners describe and compare multi-agent frameworks like AutoGen, MetaGPT, CAMEL, and CrewAI on consistent dimensions. The work also elevated **[[Coopetition]]** to a first-class collaboration type alongside cooperation and competition.
