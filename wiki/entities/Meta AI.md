Meta AI is Meta's artificial intelligence research laboratory, responsible for developing open-source large language models, deep learning frameworks, and foundational research in computer vision and natural language processing. It has released the LLaMA family of models and maintains the PyTorch framework.

## Key Features
- LLaMA family of open-source large language models (LLaMA 3, LLaMA 4)
- PyTorch, the leading deep learning framework for research and production
- Research contributions to diffusion models and mixture of experts architectures
- Open-weight model releases enabling community-driven AI development
- Advances in multimodal AI, including vision-language models
