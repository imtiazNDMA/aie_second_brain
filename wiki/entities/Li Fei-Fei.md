# Li Fei-Fei

## Identity

Sequoia Capital Professor of Computer Science at Stanford University, Co-Director of the Stanford Human-Centered AI Institute (HAI). One of the foundational figures of modern computer vision; creator of [[ImageNet]], whose 2009 release and 2010–2012 ILSVRC competitions catalyzed the deep-learning revolution in vision.

## Significance

Fei-Fei's career spans three landmark contributions to AI:

1. **[[ImageNet]]** (2009) — the dataset that made [[AlexNet]] possible and triggered the deep-learning era in computer vision
2. **Stanford HAI** — institutional infrastructure for human-centered AI policy and research
3. **AI4ALL** — non-profit expanding access to AI education

Co-author on s1 reflects ongoing engagement with reasoning-model research at the intersection of HAI and CRFM.
