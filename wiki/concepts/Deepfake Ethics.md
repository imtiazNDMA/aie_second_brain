# Deepfake Ethics

Governance concerns arising from generative models' ability to create convincing synthetic media. Encompasses privacy, consent, fairness, misinformation, and legal implications of AI-generated content.

## Key Concerns

| Concern | Description |
|---|---|
| **Non-consensual media** | Generating intimate images without subject's consent |
| **Misinformation** | Fabricating evidence, quotes, or events |
| **Identity theft** | Using synthetic voices/faces for fraud |
| **Bias amplification** | Generative models reflecting and amplifying training data biases |
| **Provenance** | Difficulty distinguishing real from synthetic content |

## Defensive Approaches

- **Detection models** — identifying synthetic content
- **Watermarking** — embedding invisible markers in generated content
- **Provenance tracking** — blockchain or cryptographic chains of custody
- **Regulation** — legal frameworks for synthetic media
- **Education** — public awareness of deepfake capabilities

## Synthetic Data Privacy

Synthetic records are NOT automatically outside GDPR or free from:
- Membership-inference attacks
- Memorization of training data
- Provenance risks
- Bias from training data
